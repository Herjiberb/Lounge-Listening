How it works

  1. Click "Capture Tab Audio" and select the browser tab you want to record.
  2. Crucial: Check the "Share tab audio" toggle in the browser prompt dialog.
  3. Switch to your target tab, start playing music or video, and click "Start Recording" here.
  4. When finished, click "Stop Recording". Your recording will load into the waveform editor for clipping and export.
